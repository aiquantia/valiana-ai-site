Valiana AI Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Valiana AI is a mobile app that helps clinicians document patient consults. It is published by AIQUANTIA LLC ("we", "us") and provided only to healthcare organizations under contract with us (each a "Practice", currently Valiana Vascular Institute) and the users they authorize. It is not offered to the public.

Each Practice is a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We process health information on the Practice's behalf as its business associate, under a Business Associate Agreement. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used and protected, and how long it is kept.

Who can use the app

Only people with an account created for them by their Practice. Each account belongs to one named person and is never shared. The app is not intended for patients or for children.

What the app collects

Depending on the features you use, the app collects:

  • Account information: your account details and sign-in records, to confirm who you are.
  • Consult content: audio recorded only while you choose to record, transcripts, clinical facts with your edits and attestation, and documents generated from them, to produce and deliver clinical documentation.
  • Patient identifiers you enter, such as name and date of birth, to identify the consult.
  • Audit records of who did what and when, kept to the minimum needed, to meet HIPAA's audit requirements and to support incident investigation.

The app asks for microphone access to record consults. Biometric unlock, such as Face ID, is handled by iOS on your device: the app never receives your biometric data.

We do not use advertising or track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you choose in iOS settings to share crash data with app developers, Apple provides us crash reports, which we use to fix defects.

How the information is processed

We process information using service providers that have signed a Business Associate Agreement with us, currently Amazon Web Services (AWS), including AWS HealthScribe for transcription. Information is currently stored and processed in the United States.

We do not sell or rent your information or patient information, and we do not use it for marketing. We do not use patient information to train artificial intelligence models unless the Practice agrees in writing and HIPAA allows it.

We disclose information only to the Practice, to our service providers under Business Associate Agreements, or when the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Consult content and patient identifiers are kept only as long as needed to deliver the documentation to the Practice, for a period set in our agreement with the Practice. Then they are permanently deleted, including from backups. The Practice keeps the documents it needs in its own records.

Audit records are kept as long as HIPAA and our agreement with the Practice require, then deleted.

The app lets you delete a consult. Audit records of the consult remain, as HIPAA requires.

How it is protected

We protect information with administrative, physical and technical safeguards that meet HIPAA's Security Rule, including:

  • Encryption of stored information, including backups, and of every connection.
  • Strong sign-in, including multi-factor authentication, and an automatic lock when the app is not in use.
  • Access limited to authorized users and services according to their role, with emergency access alerted and logged.
  • Protection of attested clinical records against change.

Patients' rights

Patients exercise their HIPAA rights, such as access to or amendment of their health information, through the Practice, as described in the Practice's Notice of Privacy Practices. We help the Practice respond to those requests.

Security incidents

If a security incident affects information in the app, we notify the Practice as our Business Associate Agreement requires and give it the information it needs to meet its own obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post each change on this page with a new effective date and tell the Practice about material changes.
