Valiana AI

Ambient clinical documentation for surgeons. Valiana AI listens during the patient consult on iPhone and turns the conversation into a structured fact set that the surgeon reviews and attests, so the visit no longer has to be reconstructed from memory after hours.

Valiana AI is provided privately to the healthcare organizations we work with. It is not available on the public App Store.
