Testing Valiana AI with TestFlight

For testers who received an invitation to test Valiana AI before release

Valiana AI is tested through TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they are released. This page takes you from the invitation email to your first test, step by step: installing the app, setting up your account and sign-in codes, and sending feedback. It takes about 15 minutes.

Before you start

You needDetails
An iPhoneWith a recent version of iOS. If TestFlight later says the app needs a newer version, update iOS in Settings > General > Software Update.
A personal Apple AccountThe account you use for the App Store. A work-managed Apple Account (one issued by your organization) cannot be used for TestFlight. If you are not sure which kind you have, open Settings and tap your name at the top; ask us if it shows your organization's name.
The invitation emailFrom TestFlight, with the subject "You're invited to test Valiana AI UAT". Open it on the iPhone you will test with. Invitations expire after 30 days.
The sign-in emailA separate email from no-reply@verificationemail.com with the subject "Your temporary password". It holds your user name (your email address) and a temporary password. The temporary password works for 3 days. Check your Spam or Junk folder if you don't see it.
An authenticator appFree, for the 6-digit sign-in codes. We suggest Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store. Apple's built-in Passwords app also works. Install it before you start (Step 3).
About 15 minutes and a Wi-Fi or mobile connectionThe first install downloads the app.

Important: test data only

Never use real patient information while testing. The test environment is for made-up patients only. Use invented names and dates of birth, and never record a real patient consult. Do not include patient information in screenshots or feedback.

Step 1: Install TestFlight

  1. On your iPhone, open the App Store.
  2. Search for TestFlight. It is made by Apple and is free.
  3. Tap Get, then confirm with Face ID or your password.

Step 2: Accept the invitation

  1. On the same iPhone, open the invitation email.
  2. Tap View in TestFlight. TestFlight opens.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.

If the button shows a code instead of opening TestFlight: copy the code, open TestFlight, tap Redeem (top right), paste the code and tap Redeem.

Step 3: Install an authenticator app

Valiana AI asks for a 6-digit code each time you sign in, as well as your password. The code comes from an authenticator app on your iPhone. This protects patient information if your password is ever stolen.

  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Search for Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, and tap Get. Both are free. Apple's built-in Passwords app also works if you prefer it.
  3. Open it once so it's ready. You don't need to create an account in it.

Step 4: Open Valiana AI and sign in for the first time

  1. Open Valiana AI UAT from your home screen or from TestFlight. The test version has "UAT" in its name and a different icon, so you can tell it apart from the released app.
  2. Open the "Your temporary password" email.
  3. In Email or Username, type your user name from the email (your email address).
  4. In Password, type the temporary password exactly as shown. It is case-sensitive. If you copy and paste it, make sure no space is copied before or after it.
  5. Tap Sign In.

Step 5: Choose your own password

The app shows "Choose a new password for your first sign-in."

  1. In New password, type a password with at least 14 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol (for example ! # $ %).
  2. Type it again in Confirm new password.
  3. Tap Set Password. Your temporary password stops working from now on.

Tip: let iPhone suggest a strong password and save it, or save yours in the Passwords app, so you don't have to remember it.

Step 6: Set up your sign-in codes

The app shows "Add Valiana AI to your authenticator app: scan the QR code, or enter the key. Then enter the 6-digit code it shows."

  1. Tap Add to authenticator app. Your authenticator app opens and offers to add Valiana AI. Confirm it (the button may say Add, Save or OK).
    If iPhone asks which app to use, pick the authenticator you installed in Step 3.
  2. In the authenticator app, find the Valiana AI entry and its 6-digit code.
  3. Switch back to Valiana AI (swipe up from the bottom edge and pick it, or tap "Valiana AI UAT" in the top-left corner).
  4. Type the 6 digits in Authentication code and tap Verify. The code changes every 30 seconds, so if it changes while you type, use the new one.

If "Add to authenticator app" doesn't work: tap Copy key. In your authenticator app, tap +, choose Enter a setup key (Google) or Other account then Enter code manually (Microsoft). Enter Valiana AI as the name, paste the key, and choose Time-based if asked. Then continue from step 2 above.

If your authenticator app is on a different device (for example an iPad or another phone), scan the QR code on the screen with that device instead.

Illustrations only. Your screens may look slightly different.

Step 7: Allow access and finish setting up

  1. Allow the microphone when asked. The app needs it to record a consult.
  2. Allow Face ID when asked. The app locks itself when you leave it idle, and Face ID (or your device passcode) unlocks it. Face ID also confirms it's you before you sign a document.
  3. Allow notifications if asked, so you hear about new test versions.

Signing in after the first time

  1. Enter your email address and your own password, then tap Sign In.
  2. The app shows "Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app." Open your authenticator app, read the current Valiana AI code, switch back, type it and tap Verify.

While you stay signed in, Face ID unlocks the app; you won't need the code each time.

Step 8: Test

Each test version comes with notes on what to test. Find them in TestFlight: open TestFlight, tap Valiana AI UAT, and read What to Test. Work through them with made-up patients.

Step 9: Send feedback

There are two ways, and both come straight to us:

  • With a screenshot: take a screenshot in the app (press the side button and volume up together), tap the preview that appears, mark it up if you want, tap Done, then Share Beta Feedback. Add a short note on what happened and what you expected.
  • Without a screenshot: open TestFlight, tap Valiana AI UAT, then Send Beta Feedback.

If the app crashes, iOS may ask whether to send a crash report to us. Please tap Share and add a line about what you were doing.

Step 10: Install new test versions

When we release a new test version, TestFlight notifies you. New versions do not always install by themselves:

  1. Open TestFlight.
  2. Tap Valiana AI UAT.
  3. Tap Update (or Install).

Always test on the newest version unless we ask otherwise. Each test version expires after 90 days; by then a newer one will be available.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"View in TestFlight" does nothingMake sure TestFlight is installed (Step 1), and that you opened the email on the iPhone, not on a computer.
The invitation has expiredEmail us and we will send a new one.
TestFlight says your account cannot be usedYou are probably signed in with a work-managed Apple Account. Sign in to the App Store with a personal Apple Account, or ask us for help.
The app needs a newer iOSUpdate iOS in Settings > General > Software Update, then try again.
No "Your temporary password" emailCheck Spam or Junk, and search your mail for verificationemail.com. If it isn't there, email us and we will send a new one.
"The user name or password is not correct, or the sign-in has expired."Check the user name is your full email address, with no spaces. Retype the password rather than pasting it. If you're signing in for the first time, the temporary password may be more than 3 days old: email us for a new one. If you forgot your own password, email us; we will reset it.
"The new password does not meet the password rules."Use at least 14 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol. Both password boxes must match.
"No authenticator app found."Install an authenticator app (Step 3), or use Copy key and add the key by hand (Step 6).
"That code is not correct."Codes last 30 seconds: wait for a new code and type it straight away. Make sure you're reading the Valiana AI entry, not another account's. If codes are always wrong, turn on Settings > General > Date & Time > Set Automatically; the codes depend on the correct time.
"That code is not correct, so the authenticator app is not set up."The first code didn't match, so setup didn't finish. Sign in again with your email and your new password, and the app will show the setup screen again. Delete any half-made Valiana AI entry in your authenticator app first, so you don't mix up two codes.
The keyboard covers the Verify buttonUpdate to the newest test version in TestFlight (Step 10). If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model.
New iPhone, deleted the authenticator app, or lost the phoneYour codes can't be recovered. Email us: we will reset your sign-in codes, and you set them up again as in Step 6.
"Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again."This is normal after a while. Sign in with your password and a new code.
The app is lockedTap Use Face ID. If Face ID fails, use your device passcode, or tap Sign in again.
"Valiana AI cannot use the microphone."Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and turn on Valiana AI UAT, then try again.
You cannot sign in for another reasonEmail us. Do not create a new account.
The app is not in TestFlight any moreThe test may have ended or the version expired. Email us.

When testing ends

We will tell you when testing is complete. You can then delete Valiana AI UAT from your iPhone like any other app. TestFlight can stay or be deleted.
