Testing Valiana AI with TestFlight

For testers who received an invitation to test Valiana AI before release

Valiana AI is tested through TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they are released. This page takes you from the invitation email to your first test, step by step. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

You needDetails
An iPhoneWith a recent version of iOS. If TestFlight later says the app needs a newer version, update iOS in Settings > General > Software Update.
A personal Apple AccountThe account you use for the App Store. A work-managed Apple Account (one issued by your organization) cannot be used for TestFlight. If you are not sure which kind you have, open Settings and tap your name at the top; ask us if it shows your organization's name.
The invitation emailFrom TestFlight, with the subject "You're invited to test Valiana AI UAT". Open it on the iPhone you will test with. Invitations expire after 30 days.
Your test sign-in detailsA user name and password for the test environment. We send these to you separately, never in the TestFlight email.
About 10 minutes and a Wi-Fi or mobile connectionThe first install downloads the app.

Important: test data only

Never use real patient information while testing. The test environment is for made-up patients only. Use invented names and dates of birth, and never record a real patient consult. Do not include patient information in screenshots or feedback.

Step 1: Install TestFlight

  1. On your iPhone, open the App Store.
  2. Search for TestFlight. It is made by Apple and is free.
  3. Tap Get, then confirm with Face ID or your password.

Step 2: Accept the invitation

  1. On the same iPhone, open the invitation email.
  2. Tap View in TestFlight. TestFlight opens.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.

If the button shows a code instead of opening TestFlight: copy the code, open TestFlight, tap Redeem (top right), paste the code and tap Redeem.

Step 3: Open the app and sign in

  1. Open Valiana AI UAT from your home screen or from TestFlight. The test version has "UAT" in its name and a different icon, so you can tell it apart from the released app.
  2. Allow the microphone when asked. The app needs it to record a consult.
  3. Allow notifications if asked, so you hear about new test versions.
  4. Sign in with the test user name and password we sent you, and complete the extra sign-in step.
  5. Allow Face ID when asked. The app locks itself when you leave it idle, and Face ID unlocks it.

Step 4: Test

Each test version comes with notes on what to test. Find them in TestFlight: open TestFlight, tap Valiana AI UAT, and read What to Test. Work through them with made-up patients.

Step 5: Send feedback

There are two ways, and both come straight to us:

  • With a screenshot: take a screenshot in the app (press the side button and volume up together), tap the preview that appears, mark it up if you want, tap Done, then Share Beta Feedback. Add a short note on what happened and what you expected.
  • Without a screenshot: open TestFlight, tap Valiana AI UAT, then Send Beta Feedback.

If the app crashes, iOS may ask whether to send a crash report to us. Please tap Share and add a line about what you were doing.

Step 6: Install new test versions

When we release a new test version, TestFlight notifies you. New versions do not always install by themselves:

  1. Open TestFlight.
  2. Tap Valiana AI UAT.
  3. Tap Update (or Install).

Always test on the newest version unless we ask otherwise. Each test version expires after 90 days; by then a newer one will be available.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"View in TestFlight" does nothingMake sure TestFlight is installed (Step 1), and that you opened the email on the iPhone, not on a computer.
The invitation has expiredEmail us and we will send a new one.
TestFlight says your account cannot be usedYou are probably signed in with a work-managed Apple Account. Sign in to the App Store with a personal Apple Account, or ask us for help.
The app needs a newer iOSUpdate iOS in Settings > General > Software Update, then try again.
You cannot sign inCheck the test user name and password we sent you. If it still fails, email us; do not create a new account.
The app is not in TestFlight any moreThe test may have ended or the version expired. Email us.

When testing ends

We will tell you when testing is complete. You can then delete Valiana AI UAT from your iPhone like any other app. TestFlight can stay or be deleted.
